About the site
BU CSE 2026

An unofficial batch hub for the Computer Science & Engineering class of 2026. It keeps question papers, the syllabus, the timetable, the class list and the photo archive in one place that anyone can open on a phone in ten seconds.

I built it because the batch's material was scattered across drives, screenshots and pinned messages. Everything here is student-maintained — official notices from the department always win over anything on this site.
